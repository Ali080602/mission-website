Campus Facilities

Providing a safe, nurturing, and well-equipped environment for our students to learn, grow, and thrive.

A HOME AWAY FROM HOME

Exceptional Residential Life

As an ideal residential institution, we place the utmost importance on creating a secure, comfortable, and spiritually enriching environment. Our separate hostel facilities for boys and girls ensure privacy, focus, and a homelike caring atmosphere.

  • Separate campuses for boys and girls
  • Round-the-clock wardens and caretakers
  • Fully Islamic cultural environment
  • Dedicated times for prayer and recitation

Everything Your Child Needs

From modern laboratories to nutritious meals, we have thoughtfully designed our campus infrastructure to support comprehensive student development.

Science Laboratories

Well-equipped labs for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology to provide hands-on practical learning experiences for higher secondary students.

Extensive Library

A quiet, well-stocked library featuring academic books, Islamic literature, reference materials, and daily newspapers.

Hygienic Kitchen & Dining

Five nutritious and balanced meals are provided daily, prepared in our modern, strictly hygienic campus kitchen.

24/7 CCTV Security

Complete campus surveillance and trained security personnel working round-the-clock to ensure absolute safety for all students.

Health & Medical Care

Regular physical and mental health check-ups, with on-call doctors available for any medical emergencies.

24/7 Utilities Setup

Uninterrupted electricity with backup generators and an endless supply of purified drinking water throughout the campus.
