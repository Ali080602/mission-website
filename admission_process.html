HOW TO APPLY

Admission Process

1

Online Registration

Register through our website when the registration window opens. Fill in all required details of the student.

2

Entrance Examination

Appear for the entrance examination conducted at our mission campus. Exam dates are announced on the website.

3

Result Declaration

Results of the entrance examination are published on our website. Only successful candidates proceed to admission.

4

Admission & Enrollment

Complete admission formalities by submitting required documents and fees. Welcome to Al-A'raf Mission!

Eligibility Criteria

  • Students from Class V to IX can apply for admission
  • Admission is based solely on entrance examination results
  • Both boys and girls are eligible to apply
  • No discrimination based on caste, creed, or economic background
  • Previous academic records must be submitted
  • Transfer certificate from previous school required
