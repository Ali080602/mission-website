Fee Structure 2025-2026

Transparent and comprehensive fee details for all academic sections and residential facilities at Al-A'raf Mission.

Monthly & Yearly Fees

Below is the fee structure for the upcoming academic session. All fees include tuition, boarding, and five daily meals.

Class / Section Admission Fee (Yearly) Monthly Fee (Tuition + Hostel) Session Charge (Annual)
Middle School (Class V - VIII) ₹ 5,000 ₹ 3,500 / month ₹ 2,000
Secondary (Class IX - X) ₹ 6,500 ₹ 4,000 / month ₹ 2,500
Higher Secondary (Class XI - XII) Arts ₹ 8,000 ₹ 4,500 / month ₹ 3,000
Higher Secondary (Class XI - XII) Science ₹ 10,000 ₹ 5,500 / month ₹ 4,000
Hifzul Quran & Aalim Course ₹ 4,000 ₹ 3,000 / month ₹ 1,500

Important Notes:

  • Admission fees are strictly non-refundable once paid.
  • Monthly fees must be cleared by the 10th of every month to avoid late fines.
  • Books, uniform, and medical emergency expenses are not included in the above fee structure.
  • Special concessions may be available for exceptionally meritorious students from economically weaker sections.
